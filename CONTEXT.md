# Research Workspace

A workspace for turning literature-backed questions into research plans, recorded findings, and manuscripts. Researchers remain responsible for scientific decisions and the claims they publish.

## Language

**Research Project**:
A bounded investigation with a topic, literature scope, research question, and research artifacts.
_Avoid_: Chat session, paper project

**Literature Scope**:
The documented boundaries of a literature search, including sources, queries, dates, and inclusion criteria.
_Avoid_: All research, exhaustive search

**Literature Snapshot**:
A dated record of the papers and search coverage considered in an investigation.

**Paper**:
A scholarly work identified independently of the copies, versions, and metadata providers through which it is accessed.

**Source Document**:
A particular accessible version of a paper, with its provenance and available text.
_Avoid_: Paper, when referring to one particular file

**Evidence Anchor**:
A traceable passage, table, or recorded observation used to support or challenge a claim.
_Avoid_: Citation, when the intended meaning is the underlying evidence

**Candidate Gap**:
A proposed unresolved question supported by a bounded literature search and still subject to contrary evidence and researcher review.
_Avoid_: Guaranteed gap, proven novelty

**Gap Review**:
A researcher's assessment of a candidate gap, its closest prior work, contrary evidence, search coverage, and feasibility.
_Avoid_: Novelty certification

**Research Question**:
A precise question adopted by the researcher that specifies what the investigation intends to determine.

**Research Plan**:
The intended method, comparisons, measurements, resources, and decision criteria for answering a research question.

**Experiment Record**:
A description of an actual experiment and its supplied artifacts, conditions, and observed results.
_Avoid_: Experiment, when describing a proposed procedure that has not been performed

**Finding**:
An interpretation of recorded observations, with the evidence and limitations that bound the interpretation.
_Avoid_: Result, when referring to the interpretation rather than the observation

**Manuscript**:
A versioned draft communicating a research project's question, method, findings, limitations, and references.

**Submission Package**:
A manuscript and associated artifacts prepared for a selected venue's submission requirements.
_Avoid_: Published paper, accepted paper

**Venue**:
A conference, journal, or other scholarly outlet with its own submission and publication requirements.
