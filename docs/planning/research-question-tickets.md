## Available route and parallel work

Start with [Persist private Research Projects on the supplied starter](https://github.com/aniketmandloi/blankfolio/issues/7). It is the only current unblocked ticket. Once its access/search foundation lands, literature status and library work can proceed independently. From inspectable private evidence, automated open fetching and manual candidates branch; generated candidates can progress alongside counter-search and manual adoption. Scientific evaluation can run alongside lifecycle and integration readiness after their actual prerequisites close.

No unnecessary dependency makes manual question adoption wait for AI generation, automatic PDF acquisition or live pilot enablement. Integration readiness, expert-labelled scientific evaluation and final capability gates are separate session-sized deliverables.
